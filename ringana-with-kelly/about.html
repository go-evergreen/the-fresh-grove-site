The Short Version

Hi, I'm Kelly.

My Story

From chiropractic school to a conscious home.

I graciously fell into learning about leading a conscious lifestyle when I attended chiropractic school with my husband. We did not grow up health-conscious at all. Once we realized that everything we put in, on, and around our bodies affects our health, we were on a mission to find the safest products available.

This came with great disappointment, of course. Either products had clean labels and performed very poorly, or products performed beautifully only for us to find out the ingredients were not as clean as we expected. We're very selective in what we invite into our home with our children and what we recommend to others.

My Story

A leap of faith that changed everything.

I began in direct sales in 2017 during a season when my husband and I were grinding to build our family chiropractic business. Living with my in-laws, our only goal at the time was to buy a house and move out! What started as a leap of faith to help our family financially ended up changing the course of our lives.

Since then, I've spent nearly a decade building a clean beauty organization from the ground up. I had no network marketing experience, and quite frankly, had no idea what I was doing. But it grew and evolved organically into something beautiful, and I am so thankful for the experience, leadership, and growth it's provided my family and me. I'm carrying that maturity into this opportunity to share something new to all of us.

Why Ringana

What we've been searching for.

With Ringana, we found what we've been searching for. A brand that puts family — and their health — first, with an expansive product line for women, men, and children alike.

Focusing on freshness of their products and sustainability at heart, Ringana is setting out to offer what every company should. I felt this excitement nearly 10 years ago when I first entered the network marketing space. I'm following my gut on this and excited to begin this next chapter.

Whether you're simply curious about the products or wondering if this business could become part of your own story, thank you for being here. I can't wait to explore Ringana together!
