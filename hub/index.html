What makes the business approach different

Sustainability, for real

Most brands add a green line to the box. Ringana built the company around how the product is made, packed, and shipped.

Made to order

They manufacture what was actually ordered instead of filling a warehouse and hoping it sells. That means less overproduction, less sitting around, and less “make it last three years on a shelf” chemistry. Freshness and waste are the same decision.

Short dating on purpose

Products have an expiration date, like food. That’s the proof they didn’t load the formula with long-shelf synthetic preservatives so it could live in a warehouse.

Vegan, no animal testing

The fresh range is vegan. They don’t test on animals.

What they leave out

No microplastics. No nanoparticles. No synthetic preservatives. No mineral oils in the freshness story. That’s a formulation choice with an environmental side: less of the stuff that hangs around in water and soil.

Glass that’s meant to come back

Skincare goes in glass with an airless pump, not a one-and-done plastic bottle. Empty glass bottles are returned, cleaned, and filled again. Reuse, not “recyclable if someone happens to recycle it.”

The wrap isn’t a throwaway carton

Glass is wrapped in organic cotton instead of a basic box. That’s padding, a reusable cloth, and less cardboard in the trash.

Shipping, as little as possible

Packaging follows “as much as necessary, as little as possible.” Where it’s safe, they use recycled materials in shipping (including recycled cardboard and recycled plastic). They have the packaging checked for recyclability and they’re raising the share of recycled plastic over time.

The Austrian campus runs on green power

The Fresh Factory in St. Johann in der Haide uses 100% green electricity. They also generate power on site (solar and geothermal).

They measure the whole chain

They publish a full sustainability report (FRESH IMPACT) with climate, packaging, water, and people numbers — not a one-page “we care” PDF.

Certified systems, not just a badge on one SKU

The Austrian company runs an ISO 14001 environmental management system and ISO 9001 quality management. Many products are COSMOS-certified.

More organic over time

They prioritize organically grown plant materials and are raising that share (their published aim is more than half by 2030).

Land they actually protect

They’ve partnered to help expand the Dürrenstein-Lassingtal wilderness area in Austria — old forest taken out of use, not a tree-planting photo op.

Ingredients that were almost waste

Some actives are leftovers given a second life — upcycled. The plant already existed. They put it to work instead of growing something extra.

  • Fruit-seed grains — raspberry, apricot, and pomegranate in FRESH scrub
  • Apple peel — in ADDS repair, to refine the look of pores
  • Sea buckthorn — the INCI calls it upcycled; in ADDS repair
  • Plankton for the eye area — FRESH eye cream, FRESH skin perfection, and ADDS effect
