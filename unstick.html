Your phone is still holding an old copy

Deleting the icon does not clear it. The reset link on the app also cannot clear it — Chrome is keeping the stuck page.

Do this in Chrome (the colorful circle), not the Grove icon:

  1. Open Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots, then Settings.
  3. Tap Site settings, then All sites.
  4. Find and tap app.thefreshgrove.team.
  5. Tap Clear & reset.

If you also see thefreshgrove.team in that list, clear that one too.

Open Fresh Grove

If a Fresh Grove app is still in your phone’s Settings → Apps, open it, tap Storage, then Clear storage, then Uninstall. After that, come back here and open Fresh Grove again.
