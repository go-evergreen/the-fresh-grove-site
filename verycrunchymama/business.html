Ringana FAQ

Asked and answered.

What is Ringana?

An Austrian family company — skincare and supplements made fresh, toxin free, with an expiration date like food. Thirty years in. Now coming to America. Independent partners share the products; nobody here is Ringana corporate.

Is this an MLM / pyramid thing?

Yes, it's network marketing — but it's not a pyramid scheme. Earnings come from real product sales to real customers, not from recruiting alone. You don’t have to build a team to earn — and the plan lets you go all the way to the top without one. No inventory loading. Fresh products can’t sit in a garage.

Where are the products made?

Austria for now — the Fresh Factory in St. Johann. $85 million is going into a state-of-the-art Fresh Factory finishing in Virginia, so the same start-to-finish care can live here.

What does “toxin free” actually mean?

No synthetic preservatives, no microplastics, no nanoparticles, no mineral oils in the freshness story. The fresh range is vegan. They don’t test on animals. The ingredient list is meant to make sense when you read it.

Will there be monthly purchase requirements?

No. A monthly purchase is never required — not to stay active, not to earn. One order every 12 months keeps your partnership active, and that order can be from a client or from you. You can run this business for $0 a month if you want to.

What is The Fresh Grove?

The founding U.S. Ringana team I'm part of — and a live community. Frequent zooms, a person in your corner, and a private hub so you’re not piecing this together from Instagram.

Do I have to build a team?

Nope. Enjoy the products, share them, or build if you want to. The zooms, the support, and the hub are still yours. And this isn’t just permission to stay small — you can actually go all the way to the top of the compensation plan with no team at all. Not common — still true.

What does community actually look like?

Frequent zooms — questions, strategy, and a room that shows up. You also get a person in your corner. Whether you want a quiet product business, a few hours a week, or something bigger, the grove is built for the version you’re building — not one script for everyone.

What is First Seeds?

The private hub this team uses. The phone above is the walkthrough — sprout, calendar, leads, Learn, clients, the tree, and messages. The real rooms stay inside the team.

What’s The Fresh Shelf?

An exclusive app for our clients and potential clients. Each partner has their own link to share. They can learn more about the products, connect with their partner, and more.

Do I have to be a “salesperson”?

No — and honestly, that energy doesn't work with these products anyway. The model is built around genuine recommendation: you use the products, you share your real experience, and Ringana handles the entire transaction behind the scenes. If you've ever told a friend about a product you love, you already know how to do this.

How much can I earn?

Honestly: it depends entirely on you. Partners earn commission on product sales and on team growth, and results vary widely based on effort, consistency, and time. Nobody — including me — can promise you a specific income, and you should walk away from anyone who does. What I can promise is honesty, a team that stays, and founding-team timing.

When does the U.S. launch?

Partner pre-reg October 1, 2026. Products for customers November 2. Founding-list members hear the exact ordering details from me first.

What does it cost to start?

Official U.S. enrollment details will be confirmed at launch, and I’ll share them with the founding list the moment they’re public. There is no inventory to buy, and no monthly fee after that. You can run this for $0 a month.

What if I just want the products?

That’s a great way to start — pick “the products” when you write me. I’ll follow up with launch timing and honest product notes. There’s no requirement to share once you join, so you can decide later.

What happens after I leave a note?

I follow up — launch timing, honest product notes, and a next step that matches where you actually are. No spam list. No script that ignores what you told me.
