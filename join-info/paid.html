The plan, in plain terms

How you get paid.

Ringana counts in points. A commission point pays $1.60. Your rank sets the percentage. That percentage is paid on volume.

Four kinds of volume.

Personal volume is what you buy yourself. It sits inside direct volume. Customer, direct, and team volume are the three the plan pays on.

PersonalWhat you buy.
CustomerWhat your customers buy. Your orders are not in this.
DirectPersonal plus customer. Your own rate applies here.
TeamYour direct volume, plus everyone below you.

Your own rate.

Paid on direct volume, and reset every month. Through Target 3 your own orders count. From Target 4, qualification wants 330 customer volume. What you buy is still paid. It stops helping you qualify.

What each Target takes.

Set fresh every month. No leg has to hold a rank. Partner counts belong to the Start Bonus, not to rank. Customer volume sits inside team volume, so customer sales alone can reach Target 10. From Target 4, what you buy yourself still pays, and it stops helping you qualify. Team volume is paid by generation: generation 1 is 8%, then 9% from Target 4. Generation 2 is 2% at Target 1, 4% at Target 2, 9% at Target 3, then 14% from Target 4, and it stays. All nine generations pay from Target 6. On that generation pay, someone under you ranking up does not cut your check.

TT-1 is team volume with the strongest team removed, for qualification only. TT-2 removes two. TT-3 removes three. Removed for qualification is not the same as unpaid. Customer volume sits inside team volume. With no other teams, nothing is removed, so those sales count toward the Target.

Growth bonus, from Target 6.

A second percentage on new-partner volume, across the first three generations, tested every month. The bar is 3,500. Targets 6 and 7 take the two strongest out of that test. Targets 8 through 10 take three out. Miss the stricter test but clear the Target 7 test, and that month pays at 3%. Someone already at Target 6 or higher under you is paid the gap between your rate and theirs.

Four ways people use it.

  • Sell onlyCustomer sales alone can reach Target 10. That volume sits inside team volume, and with no other teams nothing is taken out of it.
  • A few peopleThey pay you on generation 1. They do not need a rank.
  • DepthTheir people pay you on generation 2, and further down as your rank opens.
  • WidthMore people across generation 1, each one able to grow depth.
