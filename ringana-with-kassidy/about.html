My Story

A Jersey girl, a canoe trip, and a life built on faith.

I'm a Jersey girl at heart, now living in Northern Virginia with my husband, our three kiddos, and a whole lot of joy and gratitude for the life God has given us. My husband and I met on a canoe trip (my favorite love story!), were engaged within six months, and married on our one year anniversary. My hubby went on to serve honorably in the Marine Corps before stepping into consulting. Together, we've built a life full of faith, family, and a mission to live as healthy and present as we can.

Where It Started

Becoming a mom is where my health journey began.

When my eldest son was born, we discovered he had several food sensitivities, and later my daughter and youngest son had even more. That season pushed me into label-reading, digging deep into what was really in our food and products, and making intentional choices for my family's health. What started as survival — just trying to help my kids thrive — turned into passion. I realized how many toxins and hidden ingredients were surrounding us daily, and I couldn't unlearn it.

As I learned, I shared. That's how Healthy Habits was born. It became more than a group — it grew into a community of likeminded people who want to live well, honor their bodies, and raise their children in a healthier environment.
