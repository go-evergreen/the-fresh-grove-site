How It Works

A business without the baggage.

Because Ringana products are made fresh to order, they can't sit in stores — or in your garage. The entire model is built around direct recommendation, and it strips out almost everything that makes traditional business (and most network marketing) heavy.

01

You share what you genuinely use

Your role is recommendation, not sales-floor pressure. You use the products, you talk about them honestly, and you point people to your personal online shop link. That's the whole front end of the business.

02

Ringana handles everything behind the scenes

Orders go through your personal online office, and Ringana produces fresh, packages, ships direct to your customer, and handles invoicing. No inventory, no packing boxes, no chasing payments.

03

You earn on real product sales

Commission comes from products your customers actually order and reorder — and from supporting a team of partners you help build, if you choose to grow one. The engine is product sales, not recruiting.

04

You grow with a mentor, at your pace

Every new partner starts with a mentor — that's where I come in. Training, tools, and systems from day one, whether you want a few hours a week alongside your family or a full-time business.

Questions

Asked and answered.

Is this an MLM / pyramid thing?

Yes, it's network marketing — but it's not a pyramid scheme. A true pyramid scheme pays people primarily for recruiting others, with little or no real product behind it. Ringana is a legitimate business built on legitimate products: earnings come from real product sales to real customers, not from recruitment alone. There's no inventory loading, no required purchases, and internationally partners hold zero stock because fresh products can't sit in a garage. It's direct sales with products people actually use and reorder.

What does it cost to start?

Official U.S. enrollment details will be confirmed at launch, and we'll share them with the founding community the moment they're public.

Will there be monthly purchase requirements to stay active?

No, there are no purchase requirements, ever. You do need at least one purchase (of any size) every year to remain an active partner.

How much can I earn?

Honestly: it depends entirely on you. Partners earn commission on product sales and on team growth, and results vary widely based on effort, consistency, and time. Nobody — including us — can promise you a specific income, and you should walk away from anyone who does. What we can promise is our support, our systems, and founding-team timing.

Do I have to be a "salesperson"?

No — and honestly, that energy doesn't work with these products anyway. The model is built around genuine recommendation: you use the products, you share your real experience, and Ringana handles the entire transaction behind the scenes. If you've ever told a friend about a product you love, you already know how to do this.

When exactly does the U.S. launch?

Ringana's American facility in Roanoke, Virginia begins manufacturing in late 2026, with the U.S. market opening around that time. Exact partner enrollment and ordering dates will come from Ringana — and founding community members will hear them from us first.

What if I just want the products?

That's a great way to start — and if you feel that's the path you want to take, perfect! We'll be sharing product info with everyone who signs up as well. But we'd also encourage you to consider joining while Ringana is coming to market, in case you want to share down the road. There's no requirement to share once you join, so you can decide later.
