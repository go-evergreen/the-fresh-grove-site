The hub

Where this team actually lives.

First Seeds is this team’s exclusive, custom-built hub — the runway through October, plus the library, calendar, inbox, and client desk that stay after. The phone is a walkthrough — tap around, or tap Next.

You’re led, guided, and supported from day one. A person who knows your name, zooms you can actually get to, and a live team that stays. You’re not guessing — and you’re not doing this alone.

  • A runway, not a scramble. Your story, first products, your page, then who you’d tell — in that order, so you always know the next quiet step. Peek ahead anytime. When you’re ready, Post Studio, the calendar, and growing a team are already here.
  • Look it up in one place. Company, products, ingredients, and launch facts — all searchable, so you can find what you need.
  • Dates, drafts, pictures, and videos. Grove Gatherings and October. A vault of posts. Pictures and videos you can save. Add your own follow-ups.
  • Your page, your inbox. A lead page that’s yours. Curious names land with you. Leaders check in and send cheers — without a group chat swallowing it.
  • Connect directly with your clients. Their side is The Fresh Shelf. Yours is the Clients tab — names, notes, and a line back to you.

The Fresh Shelf is the other side of this same grove — the app for the people you share with. It’s already live. Each partner has their own link.

They can look, match, and ask. You stay the person they come back to.

  • A quiz, not a guess. It matches them to skincare, body care, and supplements — then they can look the line up themselves.
  • Look it up, then ask you. The facts live in the app. The real question comes back to the partner who shared it.
  • A line, not a group chat. Questions go to that person. Nobody else is in the thread.
  • Their link, their shelf. Their person, on a link that’s theirs. The rest of the grove stays with the team.
