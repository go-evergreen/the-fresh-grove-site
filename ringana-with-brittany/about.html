Our Story

How we got here.

01

Welcome — the longer version

We are Brittany and Tyler, husband and wife and business partners. We met in 2013 in chiropractic school and the rest is history! We graduated as the Valedictorian and Salutatorian of our class and knew by that time that we were going to open our own practice together. After we got married, we actually opened our doors 10 months later.

Back in school, we had shifted our stories to focus more on a health-centered and holistic lifestyle. This united us even more and became extremely important as we planned to start our family. We made major shifts to our household and personal care items, read labels, did research, and then became pregnant with our first child.

At that time Brittany started in direct sales for the first time — which changed our life for the even better. She quickly realized the positive impact that can be made on the lives of others, especially when the products and company are in alignment with health, wellness, growth, support, and quality of life both inside and far reaching outside of the company. She loved that people of all different backgrounds can unite and share the same passion of wellness that travels for generations.

02

The Why and the How

Now as a family of five, we extend the same level of care, intention, and research to the products we recommend to other families as we do to the ones we bring into our own home. We don't run towards something without first doing our due diligence — or perform without knowing the steps.

Over the years, we've watched the clean living and wellness movement evolve, allowing us to carefully evaluate. Along the way, we've developed a clear standard for who we choose to align with — companies that value transparency, aren't afraid to challenge the status quo, prioritize innovation, care for the environment, and lead with servant hearts and genuine compassion.

When those values unite, we're excited to share them with others, knowing they reflect the same principles we want for our own family.

03

The Steps, The Path, The Light

We value what is beautiful, timeless, and enduring, believing that the greatest legacies are built with patience, authenticity, and a commitment to excellence. The future is bright, the path is set, and our steps will continue to lead us where avenues of healing and spirits of adventure unite.

As Ringana prepares to launch in the United States, we're inviting an exclusive group of people to explore this journey with us from the very beginning — before we've even tried the products ourselves. You'll hear our honest notes as they land in our hands.

The Right Fit

It's important to us that we feel like the right fit for you.

Honesty first — whether you're here for the products, the business, or just exploring.

We're not here to pressure anyone. We're here to share what we're learning, answer your questions honestly, and help you decide if this is right for your life — whether that's as a customer, a teammate, or neither.
