Where It Started

My journey into wellness didn't begin as a career. It began with my own health.

After being diagnosed with a thyroid illness, I started asking questions I wasn't getting answers to. I wanted to understand why my thyroid had become compromised — and whether there were things I could do beyond accepting that medication would be my only option for life.

That curiosity sent me down a path I never expected.

I began researching root causes and quickly discovered that our health is influenced by so much more than what shows up on a standard lab panel. But one area surprised me more than most: the products I was ingesting, using in my home, and slathering on my skin every single day.

I started reading labels differently. I researched ingredients, sourcing, and manufacturing standards. I learned that regulations and ingredient standards vary significantly from country to country, and I became increasingly interested in substances that have raised concerns around endocrine disruption, irritation, environmental exposure, and other potential health effects.

Once you start looking beyond the front of the package and actually researching what's in the products you use every day, it's hard to look at them the same way again.

What It Became

Education and advocacy are at the heart of what I do.

What began as my own healing journey eventually became something much bigger. I wanted other people to have access to the information I wished I had when my own health struggles began.

I became a functional wellness practitioner, expanding my education into functional nutrition, personal training, and functional lab testing so I could help others look deeper and ask better questions about their health.

I research brands. I read labels. I dig into ingredients and ingredient standards. I look at what companies choose to put into their products — and what they choose to leave out. I believe consumers deserve to understand what they're purchasing so they can make informed choices for themselves and their families.

Most importantly, I practice what I teach. I've worked hard to change my own health and lifestyle, and I'm not interested in going backward. I would never recommend a product to someone else that I wouldn't feel comfortable using myself or with my own family.
