Close Messenger first

If a delete-app bar is at the bottom, ignore it. This is not done in the chat.

  1. Open your phone Settings (the gear).
  2. Tap Apps, then See all apps.
  3. Search First Seeds. If you do not see that, search Fresh Grove.
  4. Tap it → Storage → Clear storage (not only Clear cache).
  5. Go back one screen and tap Uninstall.
  6. Open the real Chrome app (colorful circle). Close Messenger first.
  7. Tap the address bar at the very top and type this, then go: app.thefreshgrove.team

Chrome’s site list will not say “freshgrove.” Look for First Seeds, or thefreshgrove.team.
