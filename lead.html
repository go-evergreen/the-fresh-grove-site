Why Ringana

Made fresh, on purpose.

Most products are built to sit on a shelf for years. These are made in small batches, with shorter shelf lives, so what’s in the bottle is still worth using when it reaches you.

Ringana became Austria’s first fresh cosmetics company on a simple conviction: what goes on your body and in your body should need no apology.

Three decades later, the founding family still leads the company — and the standard has never bent.

Almost everything happens under one roof: formula, fill, and finish. Fewer handoffs. More eyes on quality. An expiration date like food, because nothing artificial is holding it together.

The partner side

How this business actually works.

Real product movement. No monthly quota, and no garage full of inventory.

Never monthly

A purchase is never required each month. Not to stay active. Not to earn.

Once a year

One order every 12 months keeps a partner active — from a customer, or from the partner.

No monthly fee

There is no monthly bill to keep the partnership.

A team is a choice

Most people just buy the products. Growing a team is optional.

Who does what

Your side of the table is small on purpose.

You focus on

  • Using the products
  • Sharing honestly with people you know
  • Caring for customers
  • Mentoring a team, if you choose one

Ringana handles

  • Fresh production
  • Packaging and shipping to the customer
  • Invoicing and payment
  • The partner shop and online office

An honest read

This is for some people.

A strong fit if you…

  • Care what goes on your skin and in your body
  • Would rather recommend something you use than perform a pitch
  • Want the pace to fit a family, a job, or both

Probably not if you…

  • Want fast or guaranteed money
  • Don’t care about the ingredient list
  • Want someone else to build it for you

The Fresh Grove is the team behind these pages. Training and a weekly zoom are there for partners who want them. No income is guaranteed.

The plan

How you get paid.

Your rank sets the percentage paid on volume. These are the published U.S. rates, and they can change.

The plan

Four kinds of volume.

Personal

What you buy.

Customer

What your customers buy. Your own orders are not in this.

Direct

Personal plus customer. Your own rate applies here.

Team

Your direct volume, plus everyone below you.

Your own rate.

The percentage is the Target you’ve reached. It pays on direct volume, and it resets every month. A point pays $1.60.

19%Target 1
29%Target 2
39%Target 3 and up

What each Target takes.

Set fresh every month. A Target can be reached on volume alone. Partner counts belong to the Start Bonus, not to rank.

Target 1

19%

110 direct volume

TT-1 is team volume with the strongest team removed, for qualification only. TT-2 removes two. TT-3 removes three. Removed for qualification is not the same as unpaid.

Growth bonus, from Target 6.

A second percentage on new-partner volume, across the first three generations. The bar is 3,500 points, tested every month.

2%Target 6

Four ways this pays.

Your own sales

What you buy, and what your customers buy. That is the 19, 29, or 39 percent. You can reach every Target this way.

People you enroll

Their customers pay you too. That first line is 8 percent, and 9 percent from Target 4.

The line under them

When their people have customers, that next line pays 14 percent. More lines open as the Target rises. Nine of them pay from Target 6.

A wider front line

More people you enroll yourself. When they rank up, it does not reduce what you earn.

These are the rates the plan pays, from Ringana’s U.S. materials, and they can change. They are not a projection of earnings. Only a small percentage of partners reach the highest Targets. No income is guaranteed.
