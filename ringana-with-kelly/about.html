The Short Version

Hi, I'm Kelly.

I'm a WAHM of two littles, chiropractor, and wife to Lucas. I'm guilty of having a brain that never turns off. My poor husband is my best sounding board, and together we are a dream team.

When we find something we're both excited about sharing, we know magic can happen. We're thrilled to explore Ringana with you as it makes its way to the US.

Whether you're new to toxin-free living, or you've been using safe products for years, I promise you — this is something we've all been searching for.

Where I Started

From chiropractic school to a conscious home.

I graciously fell into learning about leading a conscious lifestyle when I attended chiropractic school with my husband. We did not grow up health-conscious at all. Once we realized that everything we put in, on, and around our bodies affects our health, we were on a mission to find the safest products available.

This came with great disappointment, of course. Either products had clean labels and performed very poorly, or products performed beautifully only for us to find out the ingredients were not as clean as we expected. We're very selective in what we invite into our home with our children and what we recommend to others.

A Leap of Faith

A season that changed everything.

I began in direct sales in 2017 during a season when my husband and I were grinding to build our family chiropractic business. Living with my in-laws, our only goal at the time was to buy a house and move out. What started as a leap of faith to help our family financially ended up changing the course of our lives.

Since then, I've spent nearly a decade building a clean beauty organization from the ground up. I had no network marketing experience, and quite frankly, had no idea what I was doing. It grew and evolved organically into something beautiful, and I'm carrying that maturity into this next chapter.

A family brand that puts health first, with a line for women, men, and children. Freshness and sustainability, the standard I wish every company held. I felt this kind of excitement almost ten years ago. I'm following my gut again.

What's next

Whether you're curious about the products or wondering if this business could become part of your own story, thank you for being here. I can't wait to explore Ringana together.

Why Build With Us

A clinician's eye. A family's standard.

As a pair of chiropractors and parents, we care about what actually supports the body — and what we're willing to bring into a home with children. We're not here to hype a shelf of products. We're here to explore thoughtfully and invite you along.

Lucas has continued pouring into his education and expertise on health, ingredient quality, and what products can do for the body. While I've stayed home with our children, it's only become a bigger passion of mine to build a sustainable business I can share while watching them grow into little conscious consumers themselves.

We've built multiple streams of income together, and look forward to starting this next chapter in the same Amorose fashion. If you want a founding-era partner who values honesty, selectivity, and family-first standards, we'd love to connect.

The Right Fit

If it fits, we'll talk. If it doesn't, that's fine.

I'd rather have a few people who are genuinely aligned than a crowd who aren't.

I'm not here to pressure anyone. I'll share what we've found, answer questions honestly, and help you decide if this is right for your life — as a customer, a teammate, or neither.

If you care about ingredient transparency, products that perform, and a family-first standard, we should talk. If you're looking for a get-rich-quick scheme, I'm probably not your person, and that's okay.
