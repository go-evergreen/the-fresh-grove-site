Target 1 to Target 10

What each Target takes.

Your Target is set fresh every statement month. No Target requires a partner at a rank beneath you. Figures are points, not dollars.

  • 1110 direct volume19%
  • 2220 direct volume and 1,000 team volume, or 385 direct volume on its own29%
  • 3330 direct volume and TT-1 of 1,600, or 825 direct volume on its own39%
  • 4330 customer volume and TT-1 of 3,200. Your own orders stop counting here.39%
  • 5330 customer volume and TT-2 of 4,00039%
  • 6330 customer volume and TT-2 of 8,000. Growth bonus can start.39%
  • 7330 customer volume and TT-2 of 15,00039%
  • 8330 customer volume and TT-3 of 20,00039%
  • 9330 customer volume and TT-3 of 40,00039%
  • 10330 customer volume and TT-3 of 60,00039%

TT-1 means team volume with the strongest team removed. TT-2 removes two. TT-3 removes three. Removed for qualification is not the same as unpaid. Customer volume sits inside team volume, so with no other teams those sales count all the way to Target 10. 385 and 825 are the solo ways onto Target 2 and Target 3. They are not what the 29% and 39% rates require. From Target 6 there is also a growth bonus, 2% up to 5%, on new-partner volume across the first three generations. The bar is 3,500, tested every month. Targets 6 and 7 take the two strongest out of that test. Targets 8 through 10 take three out.
