Why Ringana

A legacy that began with wanting clean products.

In 1996, Andreas Wilfinger and Ulla Wannemacher looked at the ingredient list on a toothpaste their young son brought home — and were stunned by what they found in a product made for children.

Instead of accepting it, they built the alternative. Ringana became Austria's first fresh cosmetics company, founded on a simple conviction: what goes on your body and in your body should need no apology. No artificial preservatives. No mineral oils. No microplastics. No fillers standing in for function.

Three decades later, the founding family still leads the company — and the standard has never bent.

Every product is developed, produced, and shipped from Ringana's own campus. Small batches, made to order, with plant actives at full potency. It's why their products carry an expiration date like fresh food does — not as a limitation, but as proof that nothing artificial is holding them together.

Who's Behind This

Hey, I'm Morgan.

I'm a holistic mom, wife, homeschool mama, and the girl behind Very Crunchy Mama. I didn't grow up knowing how to read ingredient labels, question what was in my home, or think twice about the products I was buying. I learned all of this the hard way.

After years of my own health struggles — infertility, thyroid issues, mold exposure, exhaustion, and feeling like my body was constantly fighting me — I started digging. What began as trying to figure out my own health turned into rethinking how we eat, clean, live, raise our kids, what we put on our skin, and how we care for our home.

Now I help other moms build a holistic lifestyle for their families too. Real food. Lower-tox living. Homeopathy. Clean beauty. Natural wellness. Safer homes. Healthier kids. More informed motherhood.

Ringana is the fresh, toxin free standard I've been looking for — made to order, honest about every ingredient, and already a yes on the samples I've tried. I'm gathering moms who want in before it arrives in America.
