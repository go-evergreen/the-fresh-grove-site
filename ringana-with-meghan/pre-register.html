    Confirm your email.

    Check the email from Ringana and click “I consent.” The account is not finished until you do. Check spam if it isn’t in the inbox.

  3. 3

    Log in.

    Return to the shop and log into the customer account you just created. A first order of $60 or more saves $25 with the code MINDFULMOMMY.

  4. 4

    Upgrade to partner.

    Hover over About Us. Select Become a Ringana Partner, near the bottom of that menu.

    • Complete your ACH and W-9, or fill them out later.
    • Select your Business Booster. It is $39.
    • Select your optional Founder Bonus Packs. They’re 30% off, and a set chosen here includes a free FRESH Date Set. If you don’t see them, continue with registration. What’s in the date set is on New partners.
  5. 5

    Checkout.

    • Proceed to checkout.
    • Click the checkbox in the top right of your cart.
    • Click “Proceed to cart with obligation to pay.”
    • Confirm payment.

    You’re in. No monthly fee. One order every 12 months, from a client or from you, keeps it active. The percentages are on How you get paid.

If you didn’t see the Founder Bonus Packs, go back signed into your new partner account. In the main menu, tap Online-Office. Toward the bottom, choose Order Starterset Business Booster.
