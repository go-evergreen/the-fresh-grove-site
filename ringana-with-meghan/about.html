About Us

Building this together.

Health and wellness is our life — and now we are building a family wellness business, and inviting you along on the journey.

The Short Version

Hi, we're Meghan & Michael!

I'm the face behind the Mindful Mommy community — a follower of Jesus above all, a wife to the most amazing husband and chiropractor, and a mama to 4 wild homeschooled children. I have the blessing of working from home doing what I love.

For the last decade I have been reading labels and finding the safest products for my family, which then led to me sharing my favorite safe swaps with families all over the country and building one of the largest organizations in the clean beauty direct sales industry.

And this time? We get to build Ringana together — as a husband-and-wife team.

Our Story

How we got here.

01

A decade of safe swaps

For the last decade I have been reading labels and finding the safest products for my family. That passion led me to share my favorite safe swaps with families all over the country. I feel truly honored to help thousands of people feel more confident about the products they bring into their homes.

02

Building in clean beauty

My passion for clean products led me to a beauty brand where I built one of the largest organizations in the clean beauty direct sales industry — over $7 million in sales and countless lives changed.

03

A family pivot

With the growth of my business, our family had the flexibility to pivot and make some pretty big life changes. Michael and I felt the Lord calling us to a different lifestyle in a different state. After much prayer we sold our chiropractic practice, our home, most of our possessions, and moved south.

We now are blessed to have our family together 24/7, working and schooling from home while building a little homestead — a dream come true.

04

Why Ringana — together

Recently we've felt God shifting some things around and sensed another big change coming for our family. Despite not looking for a new business opportunity, we found Ringana and everything in our gut said YES!

The fun thing about starting another business is this time we get to build together as a husband-wife team.

05

What's next

The U.S. shop is open. We're in the products, sharing what we're learning, and inviting people to explore this with us — as customers, or on the team.
