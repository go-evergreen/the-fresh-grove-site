The Mission

"We can do better than that."

That sentence has guided Ringana for thirty years: prove that skincare and supplements can be completely fresh, genuinely effective, and free of every unnecessary additive — and that a company can be transparent while doing it.

Every formula starts from scratch, and because Ringana develops, produces, and ships from its own facilities, there's no gap between what they promise and what you receive.

Three values have never changed: freshness, sustainability, and transparency. Everything on this page flows from those three.

The Standard

Toxin free that means toxin free.

Most "clean" brands remove a few headline offenders and call it a day. Ringana was built the opposite way: begin with nothing, and only add what serves your skin and body.

Every ingredient is vetted for naturalness and sustainability before it's allowed near a formula — with transparent supply chains and regular controls behind the claims.

Because every product is made fresh and shipped directly, there's no need for the artificial scaffolding that props up conventional formulas sitting on shelves for years. What replaces all of it? Concentrated plant actives — antioxidants and sensitive botanical ingredients so potent they couldn't survive in a conventional, shelf-stable formula at all.

The System

Four product lines, built to live together.

From your morning serum to your evening supplement, the whole routine comes from one place, held to one bar. Here's how it fits together.

Skincare · Body · Men's

FRESH is the heart of Ringana — certified fresh skincare covering your full routine, from cleansing and serums to moisture, eye care, body care, deodorant, and men's care. Formulas are built on highly concentrated antioxidant plant actives, the kind of sensitive ingredients that degrade in conventional products but arrive at full potency when made fresh.

Targeted Supplements

CAPS deliver concentrated plant power in capsule form, each blend designed for a specific need. They're the "meet your body where it is" layer of the system — built to slot into your daily rhythm alongside FRESH and PACKS.

Daily Foundation

PACKS are portion-sized sachets of powdered plant nutrition — the consistent foundation underneath everything else. The ABC trio covers three daily needs: antioxidant support, acid–base balance, and digestive fibre.

Functional Beverages

DRINKS are clean functional shots and beverages — real support without the artificial sweeteners, colors, and mystery ingredients that dominate the beverage aisle.

Manufacturing Standards

Nothing outsourced. Nothing assumed.

Most beauty and supplement brands are marketing companies — the actual formulating and manufacturing happens at contract facilities they don't control. Ringana is the opposite: a manufacturer first.

Developed by an in-house research team

Every formula is conceived by Ringana's own scientists — not licensed, not white-labeled, not copied from a contract lab's catalog. The complex recipes are theirs alone.

Produced on Ringana's own campus

Products are crafted in small batches at the company's own facilities in Styria, Austria — from the original Frischewerk to the modern campus at St. Johann in der Haide. Soon, the Roanoke, Virginia facility brings that same in-house production to American soil.

Made fresh, to order

Small-batch, on-demand production — on average about twice a week per product — means every order is made close to the moment you place it. Never pulled from a warehouse where it's been aging under fluorescent lights. Logistics, too, run through Ringana's own center.

Vetted suppliers, audited chains

Raw ingredients come from carefully selected suppliers who meet Ringana's criteria for naturalness and sustainability, with transparent supply chains and regular controls — all documented publicly in the company's transparency report.

Why Fresh Matters

The expiration date is the proof.

Ringana products expire in months, not years — like real food. That short date is the receipt: proof that nothing artificial is propping the formula up on a shelf.

Full-potency actives

The plant compounds that do the most for your skin — antioxidants, vitamins — are also the fastest to degrade. Fresh production means they arrive working, not faded.

Nothing propping it up

A three-year shelf life requires artificial preservation. A fresh product simply doesn't. The short date is proof that nothing synthetic is holding the formula together.

Made to order, shipped direct

On average each product is manufactured about twice a week in small batches — then sent straight to your door, never warehoused for months under fluorescent lights. Soon, made fresh on American soil in Virginia.

The Whole Picture

Clean for your body. Clean for the planet.

Toxin free doesn't stop at the ingredient list. Ringana's operations are built around the same principle: leave nothing behind that doesn't belong.

The REuse program: first of its kind

Return ten empty glass bottles and Ringana pays to ship them back — then you get a voucher for any product of your choice. Returned bottles are inspected by hand, sterilized in a laboratory-grade wash, fitted with brand-new inner pouches and pumps, and sent back into production for a second life.

Packaging that protects the formula

Glass flakons use a patented airless tube pump so air (and bacteria) can't sneak in and age the formula. Each bottle arrives hand-wrapped in an organic cotton washcloth — work done by people with disabilities — so the packaging itself carries the same care as what's inside.

Made fresh — about twice a week

On average, each product is manufactured about twice a week in small batches based on real orders — not warehouse forecasts. Less sitting on shelves, less waste, and products that reach you closer to the moment they were made.

On My Radar

More products on my radar.

Hydro serum and deodorant are already on my home page. These four are next: foot balm, repair shampoo, cerebro, and ADDS effect.

FRESH · Feet

FRESH foot balm is intensive moisture for feet that actually get used — plant butters and oils, made fresh, for the part of the body most routines forget. I haven't tried it yet. It's on the list because if the ingredient standard holds all the way to tired feet, that's a company paying attention to the whole body.

FRESH · Hair

FRESH repair shampoo is built for damaged, coloured, or dry hair. Fermented rice water, five mushroom extracts (including reishi and chaga), plus vegan proteins and arginine help repair from within and bring back softness and shine. A little goes a long way. Massage it in, leave it on wet hair about two minutes, then rinse. No harsh sulfates, no silicones.

CAPS · Mental Strength

CAPS cerebro is the supplement I want for the long mom days. Grape, blueberry, and rhodiola extracts, plus a patented sage complex for concentration, attention, and memory, with citicoline rounding out the formula. I haven't taken it yet — this is the one I'm most curious about.

FRESH add · Effect

ADDS effect is a concentrated booster you mix into serum — one pump of this to two pumps of FRESH hydro serum. Not for use alone, and not in the eye area. It's how you add firming support on the days skin wants more, without rebuilding the whole routine. I already love the hydro serum. This is the add-on I want next to it.
