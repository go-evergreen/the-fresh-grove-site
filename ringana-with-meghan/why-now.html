Four ways this pays.

Your own percentage is the Target. These are the other places volume can pay you.

  • Your own salesWhat you buy, and what your customers buy. That is the 19, 29, or 39 percent. You can reach every Target this way. Nobody else has to join.
  • People you enrollTheir customers pay you too. That first line is 8 percent, and 9 percent from Target 4. They do not need a Target of their own.
  • The line under themWhen their people have customers, that next line pays 14 percent. More lines open as your Target rises. Nine of them pay from Target 6.
  • A wider front lineMore people you enroll yourself. Each one can build a line under them. When they rank up, it does not reduce what you earn.
