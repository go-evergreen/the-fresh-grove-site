Why Ringana

A legacy that began with wanting clean products.

In 1996, Andreas Wilfinger and Ulla Wannemacher looked at the ingredient list on a toothpaste their young son brought home — and were stunned by what they found in a product made for children.

Instead of accepting it, they built the alternative. Ringana became Austria's first fresh cosmetics company, founded on a simple conviction: what goes on your body and in your body should need no apology. No artificial preservatives. No mineral oils. No microplastics. No fillers standing in for function.

Three decades later, the founding family still leads the company — and the standard has never bent.

Every product is developed, produced, and shipped from Ringana's own campus. Small batches, made to order, with plant actives at full potency. It's why their products carry an expiration date like fresh food does — not as a limitation, but as proof that nothing artificial is holding them together.
