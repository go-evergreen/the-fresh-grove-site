02 The question

How fresh can we make it?

Most companies ask how long a product can last on a shelf. Ringana asks how fresh it can be. The company is built on three words: Fresh. Trendsetting. Excellence.

FreshSmall batches, made to be used. The warehouse is the thing they designed out.
TrendsettingThey research, reformulate, and question the normal way. They do not wait for the category to move first.
ExcellenceThe question is whether this can be better. They have been asking it for thirty years.

03 The calendar

Made on average about twice a week, in their own facility in St. Johann, on 100% green electricity. Then it ships. No synthetic preservatives engineered to hold a formula for years. A real expiration date, like food. The first time most of it meets air is when you pump it.
